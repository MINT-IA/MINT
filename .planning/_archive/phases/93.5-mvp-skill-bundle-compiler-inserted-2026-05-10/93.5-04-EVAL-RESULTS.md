## Cost regression

| Metric | Legacy | Bundle | Delta |

| Avg prompt tokens (`count_tokens` SDK) | 10 014 | 10 758 | **+7.4%** |
| Avg latency (ms) | 8 321 | 8 991 | +8.0% |

**G-B target :** ≤−40% per turn → **NOT MET** (+7.4% measured).

**Root cause analysis (deterministic) :** the 50 fixtures in `narrator_eval_50.jsonl` carry NO `intents` field. Per D-14, the bundle compiler emits the always-on path (`compliance-narrator + life-event-router` = 2 631 tokens of bundle fragments), then `_build_prompt` appends the same 11 blocks (life-event catalog, archetype, doctrine, voice, anti-patterns, biography, commitment, intelligence, couple, language, user context) that the legacy path also appends — producing a prompt within ±10% of legacy size. The bundle compiler's −40% potential ONLY materializes when intent classification is sparse (1 intent → drops 4 of 6 bundle fragments worth of doctrine the legacy path keeps verbatim). The eval pack as currently authored cannot exercise this. Fix candidate (deferred to Wave 4 / Phase 95) : extend fixtures with `intents:` field aligned to `coach_chat.py:1020 _classify_user_intent` outputs.

| `fix-10` | lsfin | compl, doctrine, banned | 50.0% | `le mieux` | Bundle wrote « Je ne peux pas dire "le mieux" — c'est banni par la LSFin » — meta-quote. Both paths FAIL. |

**Common pattern :** the eval scorer's word-boundary regex on banned terms (`tools/eval_narrator.py:_score_banned_terms`) does not differentiate **meta-quoted** banned phrases (a model legitimately explaining « le mot 'garanti' est interdit ») from **prescriptive use** (a model claiming a product is « garanti »). Both paths exhibit this. Conservative read : the bundle path is NOT regressing; it is bumping into a **pre-existing eval harness limitation** that affects both runs equally.

## P2 Findings

**P2-1 — Denominator hardcodes 44 without gender/birth-year branch**
`rawContributionDurationGapPercent` in `avs_calculator.dart:625-638` uses `avsDureeCotisationComplete` (44 years) without a gender/birth-year parameter. Under AVS21 transitional provisions, women born before 1961 had a full scale of 43 years; for them `4/44 = 9.09%` is slightly wrong (`4/43 = 9.30%`). The "not a pension reduction" framing mitigates this, but the displayed raw percentage is systematically incorrect for a portion of the target population. P2 because the framing bounds the harm.

**P2-2 — WIRING_GRAPH.mmd missing `avs-guide → scan` edge**
`SCREEN_CONTRACTS.md §5` lists `/scan with DocumentType.avsExtract` as a `routeOut` from `/scan/avs-guide`. The added edge `EXPAT --> SCANAVS` is present but the `SCANAVS --> SCAN` edge is not drawn, leaving the acquisition branch of path A unreachable in the graph.

**P2-3 — `expat_avs_tab` Semantics wrapper on `Tab` may not propagate `identifier`**
`expat_screen.dart` wraps a `Tab` widget in a `Semantics(identifier: 'expat_avs_tab')`:
```dart
Semantics(
  identifier: 'expat_avs_tab',
  button: true,
  child: Tab(text: l.expatTabAvs),
),
```
Flutter's `TabBar` generates its own semantics for child `Tab` widgets, which may absorb or shadow the wrapper's `identifier`. Maestro's `tapOn: id: "expat_avs_tab"` and `find.bySemanticsIdentifier('expat_avs_tab')` may not reliably find it depending on the Flutter semantics merge policy. The fallback `tester.tap(find.text('AVS'))` in the existing tests still works, but the declared semantic identifier may be unreliable.

**P2-4 — UI string "44" hardcoded while computation uses registry**
All six `expatAvsCiRawDurationBenchmark` locale strings hardcode "out of 44" / "sur 44" / "von 44" while the computation reads `avs.full_contribution_years` from the registry. A registry update (e.g., for a future legislative change) would produce a display mismatch. Low probability, low harm, but worth noting.

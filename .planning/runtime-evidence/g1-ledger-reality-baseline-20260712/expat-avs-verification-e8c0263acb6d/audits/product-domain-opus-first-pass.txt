## P2
- **"Minimum" framing of the scale effect can understate compensation.** `AvsCalculator.reductionPercentageFromGap` (`avs_calculator.dart:703-707`) is a linear `gap/44×100`, surfaced as `conditionalMinimumScaleReductionPercent` and rendered via `expatAvsDocumentedMinimumEffect(...)` (`avs_gap_widget.dart:171-186`). In real AVS practice, *années de jeunesse* (contributions age 17–20) and bonifications can *fill* documented gaps, so the actual pension reduction is often **less** than `gap/44` — i.e. this is closer to a scale-maximum than a "minimum." It is heavily caveated ("no personal amount", `expatAvsTruthDisclaimer`) and routes to the official 318.282 calculation, so it cannot drive a harmful decision; but the word "minimum" is métier-imprecise. Recommend reframing as "scale-based benchmark before compensation."
- **Frontalier source-tax flat rates in the same service are unsourced/stale-risk** (`expat_service.dart:62-89`) and forfait effective rates are hardcoded approximations (`0.25`/`0.35`, `expat_service.dart:547-551`). Not touched by this diff and not part of the AVS flow, but they live one method away from the audited code and carry no year/source marker. Out of scope here; flagging for a future pass.
- **Documentation weight vs. change size.** `SCREEN_CONTRACTS.md §4.2/§5` and three `.mmd` diagrams were expanded substantially. I spot-checked the claims (CI hub, 318.282, scan branch, error snackbar, no-write) against `avs_guide_screen.dart` and they are accurate — this is *not* a facade — but the doc surface is now large relative to the code and will need upkeep discipline.

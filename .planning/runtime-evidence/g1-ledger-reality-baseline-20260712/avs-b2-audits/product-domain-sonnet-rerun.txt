### MINT Product Logic Review

The diff's core hypothesis is correct: MINT should not fabricate AVS amounts from salary + estimated contribution years and present them as retirement income. The `avsPendingStepOutputs` constant, the `RetirementIncomeScope` enum, and the Bayesian enricher's certification-aware logic all advance the DataLedger → DataQuest → Scenario spine cleanly.

The implementation gap is in the final rendering layer. Two of the four user-facing corrections in this diff (`PremierEclairageSection` certified gate, `EarlyRetirementComparison` non-AVS scope) are wired to no screen. The user experience delta is therefore smaller than the diff suggests: the B2 floor holds, the Monte Carlo is correctly scoped, and the dashboard sequence doesn't abandon users — but the AVS gap shock card fix and the early retirement comparison correction are both inert.

**Product/domain verdict: PASS**

No P0 issues. The B2 hard floor is correctly enforced across all live projection paths. P1-1 (tornado blackout) degrades product educational value and should be resolved before the tornado chart is surfaced to users; P1-2 and P1-3 (orphaned widgets) mean the certified-only AVS gate and non-AVS comparison table need to be wired to at least one production screen before they deliver the intended user value.

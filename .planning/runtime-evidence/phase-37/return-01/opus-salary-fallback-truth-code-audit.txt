The two markers are genuinely populated, persisted, and restored:

- `salary_input` provably maps to `q_gross_salary_annual` (`data_block_enrichment_screen.dart:765`), so gating its seeding on `grossSalaryAnnual` (not `salary`) is the correct, non-arbitrary marker choice.

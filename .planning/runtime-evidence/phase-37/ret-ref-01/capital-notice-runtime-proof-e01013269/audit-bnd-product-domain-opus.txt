Product/domain verdict: PASS

## Summary
This slice binds a **LPP capital‑withdrawal notice deadline** ("délai d'annonce du retrait en capital") to the strict LPP evidence snapshot (BND) as an opaque, raw‑free document reference, and adds a specialist‑handoff resolver. It is coherent with Swiss pension practice and privacy‑safe. The whole chain is gated behind `lppCapitalNoticeDeadlineEnabled = false` (`lib/services/feature_flags.dart:100`) and is being built test‑first (RED→GREEN commits). No user can reach it and no Swiss‑law constant is computed by MINT.

## Life event & decision
Life event: approaching LPP retirement, deciding **rente vs. capital**. The clarified decision is the *deadline by which the insured must notify the pension fund of a capital election* — a fund‑regulation‑specific date (commonly ~3 years pre‑retirement, but fund‑dependent). Correct design choice: MINT **captures** this date (`source: 'certificate'`, `apps/mobile/lib/models/lpp_evidence.dart:530`), it does **not** compute it. That avoids a P0 legal‑computation error.

## Mint product logic review
Advances the **ledger → dossier** spine correctly: single source of truth (binds to the strict snapshot, no duplicated variables), and the resolver **fails closed** when the underlying snapshot is replaced by a later `acceptLppReview` or when the flag is off (`document_provider.dart:453‑485`; test `resolver fails closed…`). Privacy boundary holds — references and specialist evidence carry only `referenceId/kind/snapshotId/ownerKind/confirmedAt` (+ `deadlineDate/legalYear/sourceDate` on the specialist side); the store rejects `value`/`rawOcr` keys (`lpp_capital_notice_document_reference_test.dart` allowlist test). The missing pieces to complete the spine are the **DataQuest capture** and the **scenario/PDF surfacing**, both out of this slice's scope.

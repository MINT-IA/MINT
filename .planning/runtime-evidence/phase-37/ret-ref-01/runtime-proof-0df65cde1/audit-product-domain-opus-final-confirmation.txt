## P1
**1. Stale tax baseline permanently suppresses the "add your tax assessment" re-ask (freshness incoherence).**
- Evidence: `confidence_scorer.dart` `_hasPrecisionReadyTaxDecision` + `coach_profile.dart` `SpecialistReferenceEvidence.stateAt`. The old `if (legalYear != civilAsOf.year) return stale;` was deleted, and the matrix freshness tier moved `annual → event_static` (`.planning/goals/G1-ledger-gap-matrix.md` `latestTaxDecisionReference` row).
- Repro/code path: `stateAt` returns `known` for any coherent in-force decision with no upper bound on `taxYear`. A user who confirms a 2025 avis in 2026 keeps `known` in 2027, 2028… → `taxDecisionPrecisionReady == true` → `EnrichmentPrompt.taxDocument` is never re-emitted, so MINT never asks for the fresher 2026/2027 assessment. Swiss taxation is annual; a multi-year-old assessment is not a current tax baseline. This inverts the Data Quest principle "re-confirm stale facts" into "a stale fact silences the ask."
- Mitigation acknowledged: the swiss-domain addendum §21 explicitly defers annual re-confirmation to "its own contract with its own dates" and argues `legalYear` must not be a calendar TTL — a defensible modeling distinction (the 2025 decision document is genuinely immutable/event-static). Because the feature is flag-gated and activation is separately NO-GO, this is not a release blocker *now*, but it is a required condition before either flag flips: the consumer conflates "I hold a valid reference to some past decision" with "no current tax document is needed." The separate freshness contract must exist before activation.

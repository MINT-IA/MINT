## P2 — polish / follow-ups

Align the wording.
2. **"Legal reference year" is ambiguous for a lay user** confronting a Vorsorgereglement (edition/effective year vs. applicable-law year). Low risk since it is attested metadata echoed back only into the user's own handoff prep, never computed on — but a one-line helper would improve data quality.
3. **Regulation review is self-only and requires a pre-existing self LPP certificate snapshot** (`document_scan_screen.dart:1600-1614`). Domain-wise the Reglement and the Ausweis are independent documents; a user holding only the regulation gets a silent no-candidate path (recovery handled at the review screen, but the scan-type entry gives no explanatory feedback). Consider a "scan your certificate first" hint. Partner-owned regulation is out of scope by design — acceptable, but note it as a couple-coverage gap.

Build incident — macOS Tahoe `.nosync` xattr

flutter build ios --simulator --no-codesign failed twice on Failed to codesign Flutter.framework/Flutter ... resource fork, Finder information, or similar detritus not allowed. Root cause: com.apple.FinderInfo + com.apple.fileprovider.fpfs#P xattrs propagated by iCloud daemon onto framework directories under .nosync. Flutter's _signFramework in flutter_tools/lib/src/build_system/targets/ios.dart:920–942 tries xattr -cr + codesign --force but on this rig iCloud re-stamps faster than the strip→codesign window. Workaround: pre-strip FinderInfo + fileprovider.fpfs#P on every framework dir before calling flutter build. Worth porting into tools/simulator/walker.sh.

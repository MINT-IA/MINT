## H1 — MintTextField label not programmatically associated (HIGH, PRE-EXISTING)

**Severity:** HIGH (WCAG 1.3.1 / 4.1.2) — but pre-existing root cause in shared widget.
**Location:** `apps/mobile/lib/widgets/premium/mint_text_field.dart:61-83` (`InputDecoration.labelText` not set; label rendered as sibling Text).
**Surface:** `apps/mobile/lib/screens/waitlist/widgets/waitlist_form.dart:2017-2022` (one of many MintTextField users).
**Why deferred:** root cause is in a shared widget with broad blast radius (many forms across the app use MintTextField). Fixing in 01.5 would require re-testing every form. Better addressed as a dedicated a11y phase or in 01.6 alongside the other 12 NITs.
**Suggested fix:** refactor MintTextField to use `InputDecoration.labelText` (with floating label) instead of the sibling Text(label) above the field. Add widget test asserting `tester.getSemantics(textField).label` equals the labelText.

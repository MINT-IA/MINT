## Recommendation — Codex's blockers should be addressed before execution

The Codex HIGH concerns are not optional polish — they are **failure modes that would re-introduce the exact class of bug 01.5 is designed to prevent** (data corruption + silent null coercion). Three options :

1. **Option A — Another `/gsd-plan-phase --reviews` iteration** (recommended) : feed THIS REVIEWS.md back to the planner. Planner addresses Codex's 2 HIGH + 1 MEDIUM blockers as targeted plan revisions (02-05 grandfather strategy + 02-01 sentinel-based copyWith + 02-06/03-01 slug contract assertion). Cost ~10-15 min plan revision + 5 min re-check.

2. **Option B — Inject as task-level read_first notes** : lighter touch. The 3 Codex blockers become mandatory `<read_first>` annotations in the affected plan tasks so `gsd-executor` agents apply them during execution. Faster (~5 min), but trusts executors to implement the corrections.

3. **Option C — Ship as-is, trust 02-08 panel review** : panel re-catches the 2 HIGH on the integrated diff. Risk : code already written, harder to refactor than to plan correctly upfront.

**My call** : Option A. The grandfather data-truth + copyWith tri-state are exactly the class of bug that compliance-grade plans should LOCK at planning time, not catch downstream. The marginal cost is ~15 min of planner+checker iteration ; the downside of letting them slip to 02-08 or post-merge is hours of refactoring.

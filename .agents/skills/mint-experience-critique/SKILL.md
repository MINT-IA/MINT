# MINT Experience Critique

Review as a non-author. Test five-second comprehension, next-action clarity,
empty/error states, cognitive load, accessibility, tone, visual distinctiveness,
and trust. Reject generic card dashboards, decorative AI copy, coercive streaks,
or red warnings without resolution. Findings require screen/state evidence.

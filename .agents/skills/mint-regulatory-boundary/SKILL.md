# MINT Regulatory Boundary

Classify the concrete behavior, CTA, compensation, and data flow; technology
labels do not decide regulatory scope. Identify applicable Swiss review and
required policy gates. Escalate personalized recommendations, product broking,
orders, payments, or custody before implementation. Never claim FINMA approval.
